# Shipping Policy

Effective 1 January 2026.

## Carriers

We ship with DHL, UPS, Royal Mail and DPD. The carrier is chosen by destination and parcel
size and is not something the customer selects.

## Delivery estimates

The estimated delivery date is supplied by the carrier and refreshed daily. It is an
estimate: we do not guarantee a date unless the customer paid for a guaranteed service.

Typical times once a parcel has shipped:

| Carrier | Typical | Longest we consider normal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| DHL | 2 to 3 working days | 5 working days |
| UPS | 2 to 4 working days | 6 working days |
| Royal Mail | 3 to 5 working days | 8 working days |
| DPD | 1 to 3 working days | 5 working days |

## When a parcel is late

A parcel is **late** once it passes the longest normal time for its carrier. Before that
point the answer to "where is my order" is the tracking number and the current estimate.

Once it is late, raise a carrier enquiry. Do not offer a refund or a replacement before
the enquiry is answered unless the customer is Gold tier, in which case send the
replacement immediately and let the enquiry run in the background.

## Lost parcels

A parcel with no tracking movement for 7 days is treated as lost, whether or not the
carrier agrees yet. Refund or replace at the customer's choice, and claim from the carrier
separately. The customer is not asked to wait for the claim.

## Delivery to the wrong address

If the tracking shows delivery to an address the customer did not give us, that is our
error and we replace the order immediately.

# Returns Policy

Effective 1 January 2026. Supersedes the 2023 policy.

## The returns window

Customers may return most items within **30 days of delivery**. The window starts on the
delivery date recorded against the shipment, not the order date. An order that has not
been delivered yet has not started its window.

Gold-tier customers have **60 days** instead of 30.

## What can be returned

Anything unused and in its original packaging. Items do not need to be unopened.

## What cannot be returned

- Software licence keys, once revealed
- Items marked "final sale" at the time of purchase
- Anything damaged by the customer after delivery

An item that arrived damaged is not a return. It is a replacement or a refund, and it is
not subject to the 30-day window: report it within 14 days of delivery and we cover it
regardless of condition.

## Refunds

Refunds are issued to the original payment method and take **three to five working days**
to appear once processed. We do not charge a restocking fee.

Return postage is paid by the customer unless the item arrived damaged, the wrong item was
sent, or the item was faulty on arrival.

## Cancelling instead of returning

An order that has not shipped can be cancelled outright, which is faster than a return.
Once a parcel is with a carrier it can no longer be cancelled and becomes a return.

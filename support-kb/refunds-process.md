# Refunds Process

Internal. The steps for issuing a refund, in order.

1. **Check the order status.** A PENDING or PROCESSING order should be cancelled rather
   than refunded. Cancelling stops the shipment and refunds automatically, which is faster
   and does not need a return.
2. **Check the returns window.** 30 days from delivery, 60 for Gold tier. Outside the
   window, a refund needs a team lead.
3. **Decide who pays the postage.** We pay when the item arrived damaged, was the wrong
   item, or was faulty. Otherwise the customer does.
4. **Issue the refund** against the original payment method. We do not refund to a
   different card, and we do not refund to store credit unless the customer asks.
5. **Tell the customer the timing.** Three to five working days. Say it explicitly, because
   the single most common follow-up contact is a customer asking where the money is on
   day two.
6. **Record the reason.** The reason code drives the returns reporting the buying team
   uses, so "other" is close to useless. Pick the closest real reason.

## Partial refunds

Refund by line rather than by order. A three-item order with one damaged item is one line
refunded, not a third of the total.

## What not to do

Do not promise a refund before the return has been received, unless the item arrived
damaged or the parcel is lost. Do not refund and replace for the same item without a team
lead agreeing.

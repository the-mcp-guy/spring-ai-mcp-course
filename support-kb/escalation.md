# Escalation

Internal. When to stop handling something yourself.

## Escalate to a team lead

- The order value is over €500 and the customer wants a refund
- The customer has contacted us about the same order three times or more
- A replacement has already been sent and has also gone wrong
- The customer mentions a chargeback, their bank, or legal action
- Anything involving a suspected fraudulent order

## Escalate to the warehouse

- Tracking shows delivery but the customer says nothing arrived, and the address is right
- The wrong item was sent, and it is the second time for that product code
- A parcel came back to us and the customer has not been told

## Escalate to engineering

- The order shows a status that does not match what the customer sees in their account
- A refund has been marked as issued but the customer has not received it after 10 working
  days
- Anything where the data itself looks wrong rather than the outcome

## Do not escalate

A customer being angry is not on its own a reason to escalate. Answer the question, say
what happens next and by when, and only escalate if one of the conditions above is met.

## Out of hours

Nothing is escalated out of hours except suspected fraud. Everything else waits for the
next working day, and the customer is told that rather than left waiting.

# Carrier Notes

Internal. How to deal with each carrier when something has gone wrong.

## DHL

- **Claims window:** 30 days from the shipping date. After that they refuse the claim and
  we absorb the cost, so raise it early even if the parcel may still turn up.
- **Enquiry line:** 0344 248 0844, 08:00 to 18:00 weekdays.
- **Reference format:** `DHL-NNNNN`, which is what we store as the tracking number.
- DHL updates tracking overnight. A parcel showing no movement since yesterday evening is
  normal and not worth an enquiry.

## UPS

- **Claims window:** 60 days from the shipping date.
- **Enquiry line:** 03457 877 877.
- UPS will attempt delivery three times before returning a parcel to us. The third failed
  attempt is the point to contact the customer, not the first.

## Royal Mail

- **Claims window:** 80 days, but they will not accept a claim until the parcel is 10
  working days late. Wait, then claim.
- Royal Mail tracking is less detailed than the others. "In transit" covers several days
  and is not a sign of a problem on its own.

## DPD

- **Claims window:** 28 days. The shortest of the four, and the one most often missed.
- DPD gives a one-hour delivery slot on the morning of delivery. A customer asking to
  change the slot should be sent to DPD's own page rather than handled by us.

## Choosing not to claim

Below £25 the paperwork costs more than the claim returns. Refund the customer and close
it. Above £25, always claim.

# Carrier Notes (ARCHIVED: in force until 31 December 2023)

Kept for orders placed before 2024. Do not follow this for a current order.

## Carriers we used then

We shipped with **Parcelforce** and **Hermes**. Neither is used now. An order from this
period with a Parcelforce or Hermes tracking number cannot be traced through our current
carrier accounts, and the claims windows below have long expired.

## Parcelforce

- **Claims window:** 21 days from the shipping date.
- **Enquiry line:** the old number is disconnected. Contact Royal Mail Group instead.
- Reference format was `PF-NNNNNNNN`, longer than the current five-digit format.

## Hermes

- **Claims window:** 14 days from the shipping date.
- Hermes became Evri in 2022, and the old tracking pages redirect. Historic tracking
  numbers from before the rename generally no longer resolve.

## What to do with an order from this period

Nothing can be claimed and nothing can be traced. If a customer contacts us about an order
this old, treat it as a goodwill decision rather than a carrier problem, and escalate
anything over €50 to a team lead.

# Escalation (ARCHIVED: in force until 31 December 2023)

Kept for reference. The current ladder replaced this in January 2024.

## The old ladder

Support handled everything up to €100. Above that it went to a supervisor, above €250 to a
duty manager, and above €1000 to the head of customer operations. The tiers were removed in
2024 because most contacts sat just above a threshold and spent a day being passed upward.

## Escalate to a supervisor

- Order value over €100 with any refund request
- Second contact about the same order
- Any request to speak to a manager

## Escalate to a duty manager

- Order value over €250
- Any mention of social media or a review site
- Any request for compensation beyond the order value

## Out of hours

A duty manager was on call every evening until 22:00 and could be reached on the rota
number. That rota ended in 2024 and nothing replaced it: nothing is escalated out of hours
now except suspected fraud.

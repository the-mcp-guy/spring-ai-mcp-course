# Refunds Process (ARCHIVED: in force until 31 December 2023)

Kept for orders placed before 2024. The current process replaced this in January 2024.

## The old returns window

**14 days from delivery**, for every customer. There was no tier extension: the 30-day
window and the 60-day Gold window both came in with the 2024 policy.

This is the difference that catches people out. An order from 2023 that a customer is
asking about now was under a 14-day window at the time, and quoting the current 30 days at
them is wrong in both directions.

## The old steps

1. Receive the returned item **first**. Nothing was refunded before the item was back in
   the warehouse, including damaged items.
2. Inspect and photograph it. The photographs were required, and a refund without them was
   rejected by finance.
3. Deduct a **15% restocking fee** on anything not faulty. The fee was removed in 2024.
4. Refund to store credit by default. Refunding to the original payment method needed a
   supervisor.
5. Timing was **7 to 10 working days**, not three to five.

## Partial refunds

Not supported. A multi-item order was refunded whole or not at all, which is why some old
orders show a full refund for one damaged item.
